1. Who we are

FitLens (previously called NutriLens AI) is an Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.nutrilens.calorie.tracker.ai. In this policy “FitLens”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the app. You can reach us at mokshgiri@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information FitLens handles, why, where it is stored, who it is shared with and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to these web pages.

2. Your account

FitLens requires you to sign in. We use Firebase Authentication (Google) to create and secure your account:

  • Sign in with Google: we receive your Google account’s name, email address and a unique user ID.
  • Email and password: we receive your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store it.

Your account lets you restore your profile on a new phone, gives you a FitID (a short code others can use to request to follow your food log) and keeps your follower connections and Premium subscription tied to you.

3. Information stored on your device

Most of what you put into FitLens stays in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • Profile: name, age, sex, height, weight, target weight, activity level, goal, diet preferences, allergies, preferred language and the wellness company whose products you want suggested.
  • Food log: meals you save with their estimated calories and macros, and the meal photo when you scan one.
  • Water and weight entries.
  • Today’s plan generated for you, and your conversations with the AI Coach.
  • Body Scan: the people you measure (name, sex, age, height and optional contact details) and their smart-scale readings.
  • Settings such as language and notification choices.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this on-device data. Android’s own device backup may keep a copy if you have it switched on; that is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

5. AI features (OpenAI)

FitLens uses OpenAI’s API to power its AI features. When you use one of them, the information needed for that request is sent to OpenAI over an encrypted connection:

  • Meal scan: the meal photo you take or choose, to estimate food, calories and macros.
  • Voice log: the text of what you said (not a recording), to identify the food.
  • Today’s plan: your profile, goal, diet preferences and allergies, to create meals, workouts and habits.
  • AI Coach: your questions, recent conversation and a short summary of your profile and goal.
  • Smart Insights (Body Scan): a reading and the measured person’s age, sex and height — not their name or contact details.

Each request also includes the language you chose so the answer comes back in that language. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default. See the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

6. Microphone and speech recognition

When you use voice logging, FitLens asks for microphone access and uses your phone’s built-in speech-recognition service (on most phones this is provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. That service handles the audio under its own privacy terms. FitLens does not record or store audio; it only keeps the recognised text for the meal you log.

7. Body Scan and Bluetooth

Body Scan connects to a compatible Bluetooth smart scale to read weight and bio-impedance and estimate body composition. FitLens needs Bluetooth permission for this. On older Android versions, Android also requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices — FitLens does not collect or store your location.

Readings and client profiles are stored on your device. Body-composition reports are created as PDFs on your device and are only shared when you choose to share them. If you measure other people (for example as a coach), you are responsible for having their consent to record and share their information.

9. Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications such as “someone wants to follow your food log” or “your request was accepted”. A device token is stored with your account for this and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

10. Purchases and subscriptions

FitLens Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. We receive the subscription status, plan and a purchase token from Google Play so we can unlock Premium, and we link the purchase to a one-way hashed form of your account ID. Google’s handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

11. Advertising

The free version of FitLens may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, and may personalise ads where you have allowed it. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads. See the Google advertising policy.

12. Configuration, diagnostics and analytics

The app reads settings from our Firebase database (for example which AI model to use, feature switches and the links to these pages). Firebase may process technical identifiers such as an app-installation ID and basic device information to operate these services, and may collect usage and crash statistics that help us keep the app working. If you contact support from the app, the email it prepares includes your app version, Android version and device model so we can help — you can remove them before sending.

13. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for other companies’ marketing. Information is shared only:

  • with the service providers described above (Google Firebase, Google Play, Google AdMob, OpenAI), only as needed to provide the app;
  • with followers you approve (your food log and display name);
  • when you choose to share something, such as a PDF report or your FitID;
  • if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the app.

Product suggestions in the app (for example from Vestige, Amway, Herbalife, Modicare or Forever Living) are chosen on your device from a built-in list. We do not send your data to these companies and are not affiliated with them.

14. How long we keep information, and deleting it

  • On your device: until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage or uninstall.
  • In our cloud: while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your cloud profile, FitID, follower connections, shared food log and notification tokens are deleted.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own retention rules.
  • Requests sent to OpenAI are retained by OpenAI under its API data-retention policy.

You can also ask us to delete your account and data by emailing mokshgiri@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

15. Security

Data on your device is kept in the app’s private storage. All network traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS). Access to cloud data is restricted by database security rules so that, for example, only you and your approved followers can read your shared food log. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

16. Children

FitLens is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Users under 18 should use the app with a parent or guardian’s involvement. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

17. Health information

Some information in FitLens, such as weight, body composition, diet and allergies, relates to your health. We use it only to provide the features you ask for, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. FitLens is a wellness tool, not a medical device — see our Terms & Conditions.

18. International processing

Our service providers (Google and OpenAI) may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They protect it under their own contractual and legal safeguards.

19. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent and to raise a grievance. In the app you can:

  • edit your profile, diet preferences and measurements;
  • edit or delete meals, water, weight and Body Scan entries;
  • stop sharing with a follower, or stop following someone;
  • sign out, or delete your account;
  • turn off notifications, and withdraw any permission in Android settings.

For anything else, or to make a complaint, email mokshgiri@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

20. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FitLens changes. We will post the new version at this address and update the date at the top; for significant changes we will also tell you in the app. Continuing to use FitLens after an update means you accept the updated policy.
