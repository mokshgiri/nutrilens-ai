Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

FitLens helps you track food, plan meals and understand your body. We collect only what the app needs, keep most of it on your phone, and never sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name and email when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Profile: details like age, height, weight, goal and diet preferences, so we can personalise your plan.
  • What you log: meals (and meal photos), water, weight, Body Scan readings and your chats with the AI Coach.

Most of this stays on your phone. Your account, a backup of your profile and your follower connections are kept securely online so you can sign in on a new phone.

2. How we use it

Only to run FitLens for you: estimate calories, build your plan, answer your questions, show your progress and send you notifications you asked for. AI features send the photo or text you give them to our AI provider to get a result. We never sell your data.

3. Who can see it

  • Followers you approve can see your daily food log. Nothing is shared until you approve, and you can stop anytime.
  • Trusted service providers (such as Google and OpenAI) that host the app, run its AI features, handle payments and show ads — only as needed to provide the service.
  • Anyone you choose to share a report or your FitID with.

4. Permissions

Camera (meal photos and QR codes), microphone (voice logging), Bluetooth (smart scale) and notifications. Each is used only for that feature, and you can turn it off anytime in your phone settings. We do not collect your location.

5. Payments and ads

FitLens Premium is paid through Google Play — we never see your card details. The free version may show ads, which may use your device’s advertising ID; Premium has no ads.

6. Deleting your data

Edit or delete your entries anytime in the app. Deleting your account removes your online data; uninstalling removes what’s on your phone. You can also email us at innovatroid@gmail.com to delete your data.

7. Children

FitLens is not meant for children under 13. Users under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we’ll post it here with a new date.
